Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Wetalk (https://junlinpeng.github.io/wetalk/) is a speaking-practice web app. This policy explains what data it handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account data. When you sign up with email, Google, or GitHub, we store your email address and a username. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth; with Google or GitHub sign-in we receive only your basic profile (name, email, avatar) from the provider - never your password.
  • Practice data. Speeches, scripts, scores, schedules and practice history you save are stored in your account so they sync across devices.
  • Usage counters. Anonymous per-account counters of AI requests and tokens, used only to enforce the daily AI quota.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics beacons, no cookies for marketing.
  • Your speech audio is processed in your browser and is not uploaded.
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties for their own use.

How your data is stored

Account and practice data lives in a Supabase-hosted database (PostgreSQL) protected by row-level security: each row is readable and writable only by its owner. Text you submit for AI feedback is sent to the AI provider (DeepSeek) solely to generate that feedback.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time; sessions are stored only in your browser's local storage.
  • You can delete individual saved works and history entries inside the app.
  • To delete your whole account and all associated data, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/JunlinPeng/wetalk/issues.
