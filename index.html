Features

Everything one practice loop needs

Three exam formats in one workspace - timed, scored and saved. No tabs, no spreadsheets, no API keys.

Three practice modes

Impromptu Speech, Vericant Interview and TOEFL Oral Preparation sit side by side with their own question banks, difficulty settings and timings.

AI examiner feedback included

Open a saved session and ask the AI examiner what to fix. It scores your answers against the official rubric, quotes your own words and names one clear priority.

Cloud-synced Previous Work

Every session lands in the Previous Work sidebar and syncs to your account, so the laptop in the library and the phone on the bus see the same history.

Real timers, live transcript

Impromptu runs on a 7:00 clock, Vericant uses per-section prep and speaking times and TOEFL gives 45 seconds an answer. Your words are transcribed live where the browser supports it.

Rubric scoring, not vibes

Each mode is scored the way its exam is scored: four impromptu criteria out of 5, the five SEE interview criteria out of 6 and the TOEFL rubric out of 5.

Nothing to install

Wetalk runs in the browser on a school laptop, a home PC or a phone. Create an account, allow the microphone for the session and start talking.

How it works

Five small steps, one practice loop

From creating your account to reviewing the next attempt, everything stays in the same workspace.

01

Create your account

Sign up with email and password or continue with GitHub. AI examiner feedback and cloud history are there from your very first session.

  • Email/password or GitHub sign-in
  • No install, no extension, no API key
02

Pick a mode and speak

Choose Impromptu Speech, Vericant Interview or TOEFL Oral Preparation. Draw a topic or question set, hit start, and answer out loud under the timer.

  • NSDA-style impromptu topics
  • Five Vericant sections, picture task included
  • Four TOEFL questions, 45 seconds each
03

Get scores and come back

When the clock stops, your answer is scored on the rubric and the AI examiner is ready with specific fixes. The session is saved to Previous Work, so the next attempt starts from what you learned.

  • Rubric scores plus AI examiner notes
  • Rule-based coaching tips when AI is unavailable
  • Reopen any saved session to review
