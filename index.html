Features

Everything around your speech, in one place

Writing, planning and rehearsal are one loop - not four disconnected tools.

AI speech writing

Give Wetalk your topic and key points. It returns a natural, speakable draft - or reviews the speech you have already written, line by line.

Personalised practice plan

Set your presentation date. Wetalk splits the work into daily sessions of 10 to 30 minutes that build steadily towards the real thing.

Impromptu trainer

Draw three NSDA-style prompts, prepare, then speak against a visible seven-minute clock - the same rhythm as a competitive round.

Four-criteria AI feedback

Each rehearsal is scored on organisation, analysis, delivery and creativity, with concrete fixes instead of vague praise.

Daily check-in

A short drill keeps momentum: what to focus on today, why it matters, and a one-click path straight into practice.

Your works, saved

Every speech, plan and score stays in one list, so you can reopen a project and resume exactly where you stopped.

How it works

Three steps from idea to delivery

The whole process is designed around one deadline: yours.

01

  • Or write it yourself and keep the feedback
02

Get your plan

Tell Wetalk when you present. It builds a day-by-day schedule with the segment and drills for each session.
